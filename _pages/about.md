Huaibing(Warren) Peng obtained a master's degree from Nanjing University of Science and Technology in 2025 and was recognized as an outstanding graduate student and outstanding graduate. Under the guidance of Professor Yansong Gao and Professor Anmin Fu. His research focuses on AI security, including backdoor attacks, adversarial DL attacks, jailbreak/prompt injection and data poisoning attacks. His works have been published in top security conferences and journals such as IEEE TIFS(CCF A), IEEE TDSC(CCF A), and NDSS(CCF A, Big 4). 

He initially held the position of AI engineer at Shanghai Huawei Technology Co., Ltd, specializing in AI Agent algorithms. He is currently engaged in the research of data security strategies at Beijing ** Technology Co., Ltd.
