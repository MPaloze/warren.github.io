Huaibing(Warren) Peng obtained a master's degree from Nanjing University of Science and Technology in 2025 and was recognized as an outstanding graduate student and outstanding graduate. His research focus is on AI security. His works have been published in top security conferences and journals such as IEEE TIFS, IEEE TDSC, and NDSS. 

He initially held the position of AI engineer at Shanghai Huawei Technology Co., Ltd, specializing in AI Agent algorithms. Later, he joined 360 Digital Security Technology Group Co., Ltd. and became a senior security algorithm expert, conducting research on intelligent agents for vulnerability detection.
